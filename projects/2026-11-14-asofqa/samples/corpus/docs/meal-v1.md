# Business Travel Meal Policy (version 1)

This policy sets the per diem for food on work trips. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

The daily meal allowance during business travel is $55. Alcohol above $21 per trip is never reimbursed.

Approved by the People Team on 2021-06-01. Effective 2021-07-01.
