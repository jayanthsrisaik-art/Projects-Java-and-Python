# Retirement Plan Match Policy (version 3)

This policy describes the 401(k) employer match. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Employer matching of retirement plan contributions is capped at 5% of base salary. Matching contributions vest over 35 months.

This version replaces the previous figure of 4%, in effect since 2022-11-01. Contribution changes take effect within 18 days.

Approved by the People Team on 2024-08-02. Effective 2024-09-01.
