# Vehicle Mileage Reimbursement Policy (version 3)

This policy sets the rate paid for driving a personal car on company business. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Parking fees above $39 require a receipt. Business use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at $0.655 per mile.

This version replaces the previous figure of $0.625 per mile, in effect since 2022-04-01. Mileage logs must be filed within 39 days.

Approved by the People Team on 2023-11-01. Effective 2023-12-01.
