# Remote Work Policy (version 4)

This policy explains hybrid and work from home arrangements. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Equipment issues must be reported within 2 days. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

This version replaces the previous figure of 1 days per week, in effect since 2027-01-01. Requests for remote days must be agreed with your manager 8 days ahead.
