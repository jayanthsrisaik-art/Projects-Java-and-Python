# On-Call Compensation Policy (version 1)

This policy covers pager duty pay for engineers. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Engineers on the on-call rotation receive a stipend of $200 per week on call. Pages answered after midnight earn 2 days of time off in lieu per quarter at most.

Approved by the People Team on 2020-01-31. Effective 2020-03-01.
