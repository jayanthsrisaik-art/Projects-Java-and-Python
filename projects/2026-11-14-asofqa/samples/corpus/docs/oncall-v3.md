# On-Call Compensation Policy (version 3)

This policy covers pager duty pay for engineers. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Each week on the on-call rotation is compensated with a stipend of $250. Pages answered after midnight earn 1 days of time off in lieu per quarter at most.

This version replaces the previous figure of $200, in effect since 2020-03-01. Pages answered after midnight earn 4 days of time off in lieu per quarter at most.

Approved by the People Team on 2020-12-02. Effective 2021-01-01.
