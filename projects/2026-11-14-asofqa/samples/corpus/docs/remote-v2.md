# Remote Work Policy (version 2)

This policy explains hybrid and work from home arrangements. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Requests for remote days must be agreed with your manager 3 days ahead. Employees may work remotely up to 4 days per week.

This version replaces the previous figure of 5 days per week, in effect since 2020-08-01. Equipment issues must be reported within 4 days.

Approved by the People Team on 2022-09-01. Effective 2022-10-01.
