# Retirement Plan Match Policy (version 4)

This policy describes the 401(k) employer match. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Employer matching of retirement plan contributions is capped at 6% of base salary. Contribution changes take effect within 24 days.

This version replaces the previous figure of 5%, in effect since 2024-09-01. Contribution changes take effect within 12 days.

Approved on 2026-11-04; this change takes effect on 2027-03-01. Until then the previous version continues to apply.
