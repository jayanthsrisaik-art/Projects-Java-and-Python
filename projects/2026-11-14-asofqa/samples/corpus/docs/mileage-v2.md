# Vehicle Mileage Reimbursement Policy (version 2)

This policy sets the rate paid for driving a personal car on company business. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Parking fees above $41 require a receipt. Mileage driven in a personal vehicle for business is reimbursed at $0.625 per mile.

This version replaces the previous figure of $0.56 per mile, in effect since 2020-06-01. Mileage logs must be filed within 31 days.

Approved by the People Team on 2022-03-02. Effective 2022-04-01.
