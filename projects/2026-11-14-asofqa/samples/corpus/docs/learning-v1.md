# Learning and Development Policy (version 1)

This policy funds training and professional education. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $500 for courses, books and conferences. Course requests must be approved 174 days before enrolment.

Approved by the People Team on 2021-03-02. Effective 2021-04-01.
