# Paid Time Off Policy (version 4)

This policy covers vacation and personal days for all staff. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. No more than 13 days of paid time off may be taken consecutively without director approval.

This version replaces the previous figure of 30 days, in effect since 2024-05-01. No more than 9 days of paid time off may be taken consecutively without director approval.
