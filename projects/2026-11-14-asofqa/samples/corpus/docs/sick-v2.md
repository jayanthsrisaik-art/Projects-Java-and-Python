# Sick Leave Policy (version 2)

This policy covers absence for illness and medical appointments. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

A doctor's note is required after 5 days of consecutive absence. Employees accrue 10 paid sick days per year.

This version replaces the previous figure of 8 days, in effect since 2020-08-01. A doctor's note is required after 5 days of consecutive absence.

Approved by the People Team on 2021-07-02. Effective 2021-08-01.
