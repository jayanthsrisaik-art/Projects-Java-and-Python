# Vehicle Mileage Reimbursement Policy (version 4)

This policy sets the rate paid for driving a personal car on company business. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Business use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Mileage logs must be filed within 19 days.

This version replaces the previous figure of $0.655 per mile, in effect since 2023-12-01. Mileage logs must be filed within 32 days.

Approved on 2026-11-04; this change takes effect on 2027-05-01. Until then the previous version continues to apply.
