# Vehicle Mileage Reimbursement Policy (version 5)

This policy sets the rate paid for driving a personal car on company business. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Business use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at $0.545 per mile. Parking fees above $36 require a receipt.

This version replaces the previous figure of $0.67 per mile, in effect since 2027-05-01. Mileage logs must be filed within 43 days.

DRAFT. This proposal is under review and has not been approved. If adopted it would take effect on 2027-06-01.
