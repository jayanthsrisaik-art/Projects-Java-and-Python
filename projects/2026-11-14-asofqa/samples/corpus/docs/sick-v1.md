# Sick Leave Policy (version 1)

This policy covers absence for illness and medical appointments. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Employees accrue 8 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required after 3 days of consecutive absence.

Approved by the People Team on 2020-07-02. Effective 2020-08-01.
