# On-Call Compensation Policy (version 5)

This policy covers pager duty pay for engineers. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Pages answered after midnight earn 2 days of time off in lieu per quarter at most. Each week on the on-call rotation is compensated with a stipend of $350.

This version replaces the previous figure of $300, in effect since 2022-05-01. Pages answered after midnight earn 1 days of time off in lieu per quarter at most.

Approved by the People Team on 2024-03-02. Effective 2024-04-01.
