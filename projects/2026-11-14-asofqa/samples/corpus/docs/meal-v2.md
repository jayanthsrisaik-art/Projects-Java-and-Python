# Business Travel Meal Policy (version 2)

This policy sets the per diem for food on work trips. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Travellers may claim a daily meal allowance of up to $60 while on business travel. Receipts must be submitted within 13 days of returning.

This version replaces the previous figure of $55, in effect since 2021-07-01. Alcohol above $25 per trip is never reimbursed.

Approved by the People Team on 2022-05-02. Effective 2022-06-01.
