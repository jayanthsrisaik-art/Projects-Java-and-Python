# Paid Time Off Policy (version 3)

This policy covers vacation and personal days for all staff. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Full-time employees receive 30 days of paid time off per calendar year. Requests should be submitted at least 9 days in advance.

This version replaces the previous figure of 25 days, in effect since 2022-05-01. No more than 5 days of paid time off may be taken consecutively without director approval.

Approved by the People Team on 2024-04-01. Effective 2024-05-01.
