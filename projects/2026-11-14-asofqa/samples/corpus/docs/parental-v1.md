# Parental Leave Policy (version 1)

This policy covers maternity and paternity leave for new parents. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Employees should notify HR at least 7 weeks before leave begins. Paid parental leave of 18 weeks is available to every eligible employee.

Approved by the People Team on 2020-01-31. Effective 2020-03-01.
