# Parental Leave Policy (version 2)

This policy covers maternity and paternity leave for new parents. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Eligible employees receive 20 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave must be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

This version replaces the previous figure of 18 weeks, in effect since 2020-03-01. Leave must be taken within 7 months of the birth or adoption.

Approved by the People Team on 2022-04-01. Effective 2022-05-01.
