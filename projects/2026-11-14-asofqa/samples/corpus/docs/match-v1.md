# Retirement Plan Match Policy (version 1)

This policy describes the 401(k) employer match. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Employer matching of retirement plan contributions is capped at 3% of base salary. Matching contributions vest over 36 months.

Approved by the People Team on 2021-04-01. Effective 2021-05-01.
