# On-Call Compensation Policy (version 2)

This policy covers pager duty pay for engineers. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Engineers on the on-call rotation receive a stipend of $150 per week on call. Shifts may be swapped with 2 days notice.

This version replaces the previous figure of $200, in effect since 2020-03-01. Pages answered after midnight earn 4 days of time off in lieu per quarter at most.

WITHDRAWN. This proposal was withdrawn on 2020-03-18 and never took effect. It had been planned for 2020-04-01.
