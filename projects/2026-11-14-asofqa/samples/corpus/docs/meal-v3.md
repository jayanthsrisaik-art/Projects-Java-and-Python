# Business Travel Meal Policy (version 3)

This policy sets the per diem for food on work trips. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Alcohol above $12 per trip is never reimbursed. Travellers may claim a daily meal allowance of up to $50 while on business travel.

This version replaces the previous figure of $60, in effect since 2022-06-01. Receipts must be submitted within 20 days of returning.

WITHDRAWN. This proposal was withdrawn on 2025-07-22 and never took effect. It had been planned for 2025-08-01.
