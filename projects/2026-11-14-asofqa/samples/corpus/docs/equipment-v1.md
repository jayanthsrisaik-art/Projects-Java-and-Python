# Home Office Equipment Policy (version 1)

This policy helps staff buy desks, chairs and monitors for their home setup. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

New hires receive a one-time home office equipment stipend of $500. Items costing more than $36 need a receipt.

Approved by the People Team on 2021-07-02. Effective 2021-08-01.
