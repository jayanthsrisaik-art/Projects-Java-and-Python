# Home Office Equipment Policy (version 2)

This policy helps staff buy desks, chairs and monitors for their home setup. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Purchases must be claimed within 70 days of the start date. A one-time home office equipment stipend of $600 is provided to each new hire.

This version replaces the previous figure of $500, in effect since 2021-08-01. Items costing more than $53 need a receipt.

Approved by the People Team on 2023-05-02. Effective 2023-06-01.
