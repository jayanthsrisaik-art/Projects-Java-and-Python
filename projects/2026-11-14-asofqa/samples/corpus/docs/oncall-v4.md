# On-Call Compensation Policy (version 4)

This policy covers pager duty pay for engineers. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Shifts may be swapped with 3 days notice. Engineers on the on-call rotation receive a stipend of $300 per week on call.

This version replaces the previous figure of $250, in effect since 2021-01-01. Shifts may be swapped with 4 days notice.

Approved by the People Team on 2022-04-01. Effective 2022-05-01.
