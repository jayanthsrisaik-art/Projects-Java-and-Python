# Learning and Development Policy (version 4)

This policy funds training and professional education. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Unused budget up to $249 may roll over once. An annual learning budget of $3,000 per employee covers courses, books and conferences.

This version replaces the previous figure of $2,000, in effect since 2023-12-01. Course requests must be approved 232 days before enrolment.

Approved by the People Team on 2024-10-02. Effective 2024-11-01.
