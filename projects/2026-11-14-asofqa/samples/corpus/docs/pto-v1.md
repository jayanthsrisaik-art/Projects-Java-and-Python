# Paid Time Off Policy (version 1)

This policy covers vacation and personal days for all staff. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Requests should be submitted at least 6 days in advance. Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid time off per calendar year.

Approved by the People Team on 2021-07-02. Effective 2021-08-01.
