# Retirement Plan Match Policy (version 2)

This policy describes the 401(k) employer match. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Employer matching of retirement plan contributions is capped at 4% of base salary. Matching contributions vest over 26 months.

This version replaces the previous figure of 3%, in effect since 2021-05-01. Contribution changes take effect within 33 days.

Approved by the People Team on 2022-10-02. Effective 2022-11-01.
