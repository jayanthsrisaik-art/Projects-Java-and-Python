# Learning and Development Policy (version 3)

This policy funds training and professional education. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Unused budget up to $322 may roll over once. Each employee has an annual learning budget of $2,000 for courses, books and conferences.

This version replaces the previous figure of $1,500, in effect since 2022-11-01. Unused budget up to $114 may roll over once.

Approved by the People Team on 2023-11-01. Effective 2023-12-01.
