# Remote Work Policy (version 3)

This policy explains hybrid and work from home arrangements. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Equipment issues must be reported within 2 days. Employees may work remotely up to 1 days per week.

This version replaces the previous figure of 4 days per week, in effect since 2022-10-01. Requests for remote days must be agreed with your manager 4 days ahead.

Approved on 2026-11-02; this change takes effect on 2027-01-01. Until then the previous version continues to apply.
