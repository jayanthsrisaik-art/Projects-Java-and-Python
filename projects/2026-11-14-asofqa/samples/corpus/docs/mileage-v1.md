# Vehicle Mileage Reimbursement Policy (version 1)

This policy sets the rate paid for driving a personal car on company business. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Mileage driven in a personal vehicle for business is reimbursed at $0.56 per mile. Mileage logs must be filed within 16 days.

Approved by the People Team on 2020-05-02. Effective 2020-06-01.
