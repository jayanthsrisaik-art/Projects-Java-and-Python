# Sick Leave Policy (version 3)

This policy covers absence for illness and medical appointments. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Every employee accrues 15 paid sick days each year. A doctor's note is required after 4 days of consecutive absence.

This version replaces the previous figure of 10 days, in effect since 2021-08-01. Up to 3 days may be used to care for a family member.

DRAFT. This proposal is under review and has not been approved. If adopted it would take effect on 2027-03-01.
