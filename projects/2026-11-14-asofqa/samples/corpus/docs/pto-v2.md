# Paid Time Off Policy (version 2)

This policy covers vacation and personal days for all staff. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

No more than 12 days of paid time off may be taken consecutively without director approval. Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off per calendar year.

This version replaces the previous figure of 15 days, in effect since 2021-08-01. No more than 7 days of paid time off may be taken consecutively without director approval.

Approved by the People Team on 2022-04-01. Effective 2022-05-01.
