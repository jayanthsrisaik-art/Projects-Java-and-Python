# Remote Work Policy (version 1)

This policy explains hybrid and work from home arrangements. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Staff are permitted to work remotely on as many as 5 days per week. Requests for remote days must be agreed with your manager 9 days ahead.

Approved by the People Team on 2020-07-02. Effective 2020-08-01.
