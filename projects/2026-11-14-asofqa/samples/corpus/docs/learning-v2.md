# Learning and Development Policy (version 2)

This policy funds training and professional education. It applies to all employees of Northwind Labs.

Unused budget up to $380 may roll over once. An annual learning budget of $1,500 per employee covers courses, books and conferences.

This version replaces the previous figure of $500, in effect since 2021-04-01. Unused budget up to $107 may roll over once.

Approved by the People Team on 2022-10-02. Effective 2022-11-01.
